## What was deployed

`src/userplugins/spatialVoice` copied from this project into the checkout, then `pnpm build`,
then `dist/.` copied into `%APPDATA%\Vencord\dist`. Vencord keys a userplugin's settings by
its display name, so the persistent switch lives at `plugins["Spatial Voice"].enabled`
(currently `true`, with its own option block) and not at `plugins.spatialVoice`; the
`spatialVoice` key written at install time was a sibling Vencord discarded on its next save.
Unrelated settings, `eslint.config.mjs` and `package.json` were left alone.

One source change was made during deployment, in my own two files:
`style.css` and `callFocus.ts` raised the chevron from `z-index: 30` to `1100`, and
`callFocus.style.test.ts` asserts it stays above 1001. Reason, measured: Discord's call
header lives in a `z-index: 1001` layer (`videoControls`) inside the same call root, so at 30
the chevron painted under the header and lost the hit test in the windowed call view; a real
pointer at the chevron's own centre resolved to the header's icon and the press never reached
the button.

## Defects found in the deployed build (not fixed here)

1. Leaving Discord's fullscreen tears the scene down for several seconds. Measured after a
   press: `hosts` 1 → 0 → 1 again after about 8 s, `toggles` 1 → 0 → 1 with it. Discord
   rebuilds the call view during the transition, the adapter's look finds no `[data-call-tile]`
   and unmounts, and the scene only returns on a later mutation-driven look.
2. The chevron sits over Discord's "Go back" control in the windowed call view (both at
   432..464, 44..76). With `z-index: 1100` the chevron now takes the press; Discord's back
   arrow underneath it does not.
3. In one 8 s window while the call was live, the chevron's own handler fired three times
   (`Full Screen`, `Exit Full Screen`, `Full Screen`), so fullscreen entered, left and
   re-entered. Some of that may be the person in the call pressing the control; the plugin
   also presses it itself when a look sees no call view, which is defect 1's path.
4. `Page.captureScreenshot` timed out while Discord's call fullscreen was on, so the only
   fullscreen-state screenshot is `02-native-fullscreen.png`, captured earlier on the same
   installed build before the z-index change; the windowed and Friends captures are current.
