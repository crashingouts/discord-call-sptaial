Call focus toggle: both states, the same stylesheet

Every control on this page is one .sv-call-focus-toggle from style.css: this page adds no rule of its own for it, so what you see is what the plugin paints. Hover a control for its hover state and press Tab from the top of the page to reach the focus ring. The two panels on the first row put the control where the screenshot has it, in Discord's own chrome, outside the scene, in the header's own row before the participant pill (data-sv-placement="inline"). The second row puts it in a scene, at the corner of its host (the default placement), where the theme tokens replace the fallbacks.
